Wait With Me — Support

A visual timer for waiting

The app has no words in it, which makes it quick to learn and occasionally puzzling for a minute. Here is everything it does.

Using it

Setting the time
Drag your finger around the outer ring. The coloured wedge follows. Up to sixty minutes — one full turn of the dial.
Starting and pausing
Tap the circle in the middle. Tap it again to pause.
Why the wedge is small
The dial is always an hour, like a clock face. Ten minutes is a sixth of it. That is deliberate — it means a big wedge always looks like a long wait, and the tick marks always mean the same thing.
Changing the time while it runs
You cannot, on purpose. Pause first. It stops a child adding twenty minutes to their own waiting by brushing the screen.
The last ten seconds
The middle of the dial counts down — 10, 9, 8 — with a soft tic-toc on each number, because by then the wedge is too thin to see. At zero, a gentle three-note chime plays twice. It is meant to sound like arriving, not like an alarm.
The buttons underneath
Reset · the tic-toc · the chime · the numbers. The four colours are on the row below. Everything is remembered.
Which switch is on?
A switch that is on is bright. A switch that is off is dimmed and has a line through it — the same as a mute button. The metronome is the tic-toc; the bell is the chime. Turn the tic-toc off and keep the chime, for a child who finds ticking hard.

Common questions

It did not make a sound
Check the bell has no line through it, and that the volume is up. The chime plays even when the phone is on silent, like a clock's timer — it is the signal that the wait is over. It never stops music that is already playing.
The screen went dark mid-wait
The app asks the device to stay awake while it is open. If the screen still sleeps, check Auto-Lock in your device settings.
Does it work without internet?
Always. The app cannot use the internet at all — on Android it holds no internet permission, so the operating system prevents it.
Is there anything to buy?
No. It is free, with no advertising, no subscription and nothing to unlock. There is no paid version.
What does it collect about my child?
Nothing at all. See the privacy policy.
